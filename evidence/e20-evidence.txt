MECHANISM (this phase's own work)
====================================
New unierp-api/src/modules/hr/employee-pii-encryption.service.ts:
AES-256-GCM, key derived per-tenant via scrypt from a server-side
master secret (PII_ENCRYPTION_MASTER_KEY) — no database storage, no
migration needed (none available in this checkout; schema lives in
the separate @unerp/database package).

  encryptBankDetails(tenantId, bankDetails) -> ciphertext envelope
    { __encrypted: true, ciphertext, iv, authTag, tenantId }
  decryptBankDetails(tenantId, envelope) -> original object
    REFUSES (ForbiddenException) if the envelope's own recorded
    tenantId doesn't match the caller's tenant — tenant isolation on
    the encryption key itself, not just the row-level query.

Wired into HrService.createEmployee/updateEmployee — bankDetails is
encrypted before every write. New getEmployeeBankDetails() is the
ONLY decrypt path, called on demand rather than transparently on
every read, keeping decrypted PII out of unrelated list/search
response payloads by construction.

dateOfBirth is DELIBERATELY left unencrypted — hr.service.ts already
queries it directly (a real, working upcoming-birthday feature at
line ~2153). Encrypting it would need searchable/deterministic
encryption or a decrypt-then-filter rewrite of that feature — a
larger, separately-scoped change, stated as a gap rather than
silently worked around or left unaddressed without comment.
