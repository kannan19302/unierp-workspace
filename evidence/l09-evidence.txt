MECHANISM 2 — one complete decomposition as proof of technique
====================================================================
projects/portfolios/page.tsx (301 lines, the smallest real violator)
had two clean, obviously-separable pieces: a data-fetch/create flow
(useState/useEffect/fetch/POST) and two distinct render blocks (a
portfolio rollup card, and a "New Portfolio" create form). Extracted:

  hooks/use-portfolios.ts     - usePortfolios() hook: fetch, loading/
                                 error state, createPortfolio()
  components/portfolio-card.tsx        - one portfolio's rollup card
  components/create-portfolio-modal.tsx - the create form

page.tsx is now 61 lines: state (isModalOpen only), the hook call, and
composition of the two sub-components.

REUSABILITY ASSESSMENT — stated, not assumed
=================================================
Neither extracted component was moved to unierp-design-system.
portfolio-card.tsx and create-portfolio-modal.tsx are both shaped
entirely around the Portfolio entity (strategic alignment enum,
budget/risk KPI fields specific to portfolios) — not generic reusable
primitives another page would compose. The exit criterion's own
wording is conditional ("components that ARE reusable land in
design-system"), not "every extraction must produce a design-system
component" — forcing a non-generic component into the shared package
would itself be the wrong move.
